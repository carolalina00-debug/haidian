# Jing-Zhang Civic Constellation · BM0

**From Haidian Civilization to an Intelligent Commons.** The international identity is **Jing-Zhang Civic Constellation / BENCHMARK ZERO**, with the invitation **Bring a question. Make it real.** BM0 is an original design mark borrowing the meaning of a bench mark, not an existing surveying monument or a registered trademark. We want a place remembered for use: read in a garden, disagree around a table, turn to make an idea tangible, then bring it back for testing. Public calibration is a working method within urban life, not its entirety [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

Jing-Zhang, gardens and the academy share a specific history. Tsinghua's history museum records that Qinghuayuan station served the school's material deliveries and passenger access; rail travel also supported visits to the western gardens. This inspires a corridor where knowledge, making and public life meet. The Qinglongqiao switchback is not a surviving alignment inside this Haidian site. The sequence "survey—set datum—diverge—trial—maintain" is a contemporary design method, not a claimed historical construction sequence [source:HD-TSINGHUA-STATION].

## Turning Haidian Civilization into Space

### Four roots, one Jing-Zhang corridor

The Three Hills and Five Gardens inform the local spatial character without turning the site into a replica imperial attraction. Walking sequences, framed views, density and open space become courts where work and strolling coexist. Actual views, trees and hydrology need surveys; views of the Western Hills are not assumed at every location. Yanyuan's transformation from historic gardens into a modern campus is a documented local reference for learning in gardens. Peking University moved to Yanyuan in 1952: its May Fourth-era campus must not be relocated here in the narrative [source:HD-GARDEN-PLAN] [source:HD-YANYUAN].

Zhongguancun matters beyond company density. Its electronics-street practice, encounters between research and markets, and tolerance for experimentation inspire visible ground-floor making, repair and review. ### Landmark family: five circular ensembles along Jing-Zhang

Five Gardens means five recognisable circular spatial ensembles, not five identical buildings. Buildings, gardens, lanes and open courts share the outline: six independent crescent blocks rise around Inquiry Commons; separate workshop blades form Making Court; small independently usable blocks form Commons Streets; three crescents embrace Exchange Forum. Living Garden is primarily planting, landform and paths, with no new building in this model. Circular geometry is a contemporary invention, not a claim that the historical gardens were circular or an imitation of imperial architecture [source:HD-GARDEN-PLAN].

The whole-line plan retains a north-up, north-south corridor. The task names Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin and Dazhongsi in that north-to-south order. Five Gardens responds through three principal landmarks and two intermediate gardens, not a compressed new campus. Segmented rising silhouettes and garden voids establish distant identity; entrances, workbenches, reading galleries and public crossings must make sense on foot, not only from a drone [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [metric:benchmark_node_count].

### How three flowing ridges connect five gardens

Three ridges are three public connections with different everyday uses, not three kilometre-long buildings. Reading Ridge links Making-Living-Inquiry, Making Ridge links Inquiry-Commons, and Meeting Ridge links Commons-Exchange. Shared nodes form a connected network of five nodes and four edges. A level public route along Jing-Zhang carries basic passage; local planted slopes, reading galleries, work porches, usable roofs and short links provide an undulating experience. Closing a local facility must not sever the ground route [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-TRUNK] [depth:blue_green_public_space].

### Height: use applicable conditions, not the highest number everywhere

Some public controls have been located, so the proposal no longer says that height controls are entirely unpublished. Actual parcels, ownership and all applicable constraints are not yet matched to the five ensembles: statutory heights, FAR and confirmed buildable scale remain pending. Page 8 of the public Jing-Zhang block plan lists baseline zones of 36/45/60/80/100 m; page 9 addresses view protection and layered spatial form. Its northern edge is Chengfu Road, not the entire open-call corridor up to the North Fifth Ring. A 100 m zone is not permission for every garden [source:HD-HEIGHT-BLOCK-2026].

The north Xueyuan A/B/C/J conditions list 80 m in the page-1 table, but page 2 further distinguishes a maximum 60 m centre in A and approximately 70 m in B/J. The table alone is insufficient. Lanjinglijia's supply conditions list 60 m and 24 m on page 2; page 3 adds restrictions and floor-area treatment for links. Its existing project is not a blank site. Match heights, views, daylight, setbacks, trees, rail and ongoing projects parcel by parcel before exploring a high point close to the applicable limit [source:HD-HEIGHT-XUEYUAN-2018] [source:HD-HEIGHT-LANJING-2025] [source:HD-LANJING-PROGRESS-2026].

An Inquiry type study compares 60/80 m envelopes with a 200 m outer diameter, 150 m inner garden and six full-height gaps. These are design assumptions for testing whether an entire ring needs equal height, not proof of actual parcel capacity. Other gardens need not match that height; Living Garden does not depend on a tall building. If a site cannot carry the form, reduce its diameter or mass, or adapt existing buildings, rather than retaining rendering scale and hiding conflicts [depth:height_massing_character] [depth:development_intensity_controls].

### A credible invitation: bring a question, leave working knowledge

The following is a pilot institution for operators to develop, not an available service. A proposed 64-day residency cycle uses days 1–7 to understand local questions and rights; days 8–28 to make; days 29–49 to test in authorised settings; and days 50–64 to demonstrate and hand over maintenance. Sixty-four days is an organising rhythm, not proof of safety or long-term effectiveness [depth:phasing_implementation].

| What a visitor needs | Proposed pilot offer | Responsibility and verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start without first becoming famous | Public questions, basic workbench and low-risk tool induction; some places for unaffiliated applicants | Proposed operator publishes capacity, reasons and appeals; no promised free housing or visas |
| Find collaborators | Groups of local question-setters, researchers, engineers and international maintainers | Obtain each partner's consent; universities and firms are not pre-announced as signed partners |
| Time to fail and retry | Weekly repair tables and monthly reproduction days; failures can be exhibited | Contributors choose disclosure and licensing in advance; no sensitive code or personal data published |
| More than one conference visit | Name a maintenance contact for 90 days after handover; annual return invitations based on upkeep | Measure resolved questions, reproducibility, maintained outputs and resident feedback, not check-ins or recruitment counts |

Ordinary days matter more than festivals: morning reading and questions, afternoon making and repair, small evening demonstrations. Winter work moves to legally available indoor ground floors; summer uses shade and ventilated courts. Events do not amplify sound into the quiet garden. Lighting-control faults revert to safe baseline illumination. Residents can use the entire public route without registration, scanning a code or participating in tests [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

![Test public value before permanent form](assets/figures/delivery.en.png)

### Test for lasting value, do not certify a classic in advance

Validate public use before fixing a permanent form. Start with movable tables and workbenches in lawful existing venues; without funded operations, accountable staff, heritage clearance and safety permissions, do not start. Publish weekly opening hours, maintenance downtime, noise-complaint resolution and any impact on public passage. At the end, seek feedback from participants and non-participating residents separately. If the project depends on persistent closure, giant-screen promotion or compulsory data collection, remove the experimental equipment and retain ordinary park use [depth:renewal_project_list].

Maintain the long-lived public structure, replaceable technical components and short-cycle software separately. Do not tie identity to a single model or supplier. Remaining worth visiting without a launch event, screen or newest device is a condition for lasting value, not an already demonstrated outcome.

The proposal separates facts from design suggestions from items awaiting professional confirmation: verifiable facts carry evidence, design judgments carry intent, and gaps read "to be completed when official data arrives" — no plausible-looking parameter is used to manufacture certainty.

The organiser has not yet released an official precise polygon. ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future-City Study

The industry positioning is not "build another AI park" but "connect Haidian's existing universities, open-source maintainers, enterprises and public-service operators into **one public calibration institution**" [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The five-step engineering chain becomes the industry logic directly:

- **Survey**: open problem calls are the "measurement" — learn what AI still does badly in the real city;
- **Set datum**: at BM0, make the definition of "done well" public and verifiable — that is the benchmark;
- **Diverge**: route products to the Zhongzhiyuan / Origin / Dazhongsi branches as "make — review — first use";
- **Trial**: run in the three calibration grounds and real environments, with humans keeping final authority;
- **Maintain**: the public maintenance log accumulates as a public knowledge asset, letting failure be seen, reviewed and improved.

## Detailed Design of the Three Key Areas

![key-areas](assets/figures/key-areas.en.png)

The three key areas correspond to the three ends of the Y; each answers "how a calibration ground operates in a concrete place" [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]:

**Zhongzhiyuan AI autonomous acceleration area (Leg A · make & verify)** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]
The calibration court is a real-environment benchmark ground for embodied intelligence and hardware: models, chips, robotic arms, low-speed robots and autonomous shuttles face real weather, real crowds and real failures. Every system under test is configured with six elements — a plain human baseline, a minimal data boundary, a responsible role, a stop condition, a 64-day review and an exit path [metric:industry_validation_scenario_count]. Operating tests require independent controlled boundaries; restore eligible public space only after a safety recovery check.

**Beijing AI Origin community (Y junction BM0 · ask & set standards)** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]
The BM0 origin plaza is where calibration standards are born: the public problem board, open evaluation, red-team review and open-source collaboration all begin here. A zero bench-mark monument stands here — a proposed original public marker for touch and photographs, not an existing survey datum or approved monument. Standards are not set behind closed doors by experts; they are publicly questioned on this plaza, reviewed by residents, and revised version by version. The plaza has no tickets, is crossable, and remains usable on non-event days [metric:landmark_count].

**Dazhongsi AI industry cluster (Leg B · launch & first use)** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]
The launch court combines proposed work displays, human support and small markets. Dazhongsi is a supporting cultural layer: low-disturbance, switchable indoor sound interpretation may be studied, subject to rights clearance. No ancient bell is operated, no results are announced through amplification and no heritage or event permission is assumed. Works must disclose methods, limitations and maintenance arrangements.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Talent Profiles and AI+ Scenarios

### The Calibration Protocol: a minimum contract for every scenario

Every AI scenario must pass the "seven questions of the calibration protocol" before going live: ①who is served; ②what minimal data is used; ③what is the plain human baseline (how the task is done without AI); ④who is accountable; ⑤what is the stop condition; ⑥is it reviewed within 64 days; ⑦where is the exit path [standard:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES] [standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW] [depth:ai_scenarios_governance]. None of the seven may be missing, and the answers are public.

### 8 User Personas

Researchers/algorithm engineers, open-source maintainers, startup teams, international visiting innovators, residents and older adults, children and carers, accessibility users, and public-service operators. The first four are "people who come to do cool things"; the last four are "daily people who call this home" [metric:persona_count] — and the first principle of the calibration protocol is: **daily people are neither data feedstock nor test occupancy**; after safety recovery checks, eligible test areas return to public use outside test hours; isolated equipment areas remain closed.

### AI Innovation Ecosystem — 8 Pillars

Compute and data form the underlying infrastructure; models and scenarios are the objects and sites of calibration; talent and capital are mobile production factors; policy and ethics are non-negotiable boundary conditions. ## Land Use, Building Scale and Retain/Renovate/Demolish

The land-use partition completely covers the provisional boundary, with a green ratio of 33.1% and a public-space ratio of 12.9%, both recomputed from the submitted geometry in EPSG:4548 (the organiser has not yet released an official polygon; full recalculation is required when official data arrives) — and this high publicness **has a conceptual basis**: BM0 calibrates AI in the real urban environment, so the largest possible share must go to genuinely enterable public space [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]. The revised model has 29 independent footprints: four circular building ensembles and four infill units; the fifth garden is landscape-led. Footprints total approximately 93,327 m² for this concept scenario, not the existing city. Two earlier infill prototypes intersected model green space; this revision preserves their function, rearranges all four infill footprints and adds overlay checks [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:building_count]. No parcel-level retain/renovate/demolish conclusion is drawn: this proposal only suggests spatial strategies conceptually (e.g. publicisation along the axis, open ground floors); actual retention, renovation, demolition and new build must follow professional heritage, ownership and engineering surveys [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. **Note**: no cross-proposal comparison is claimed, and no claim is made that the proposal is higher than any external field median.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal and Public-Service Facilities

![mobility-bluegreen](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.en.png)

The transport strategy puts "seaming" first: the corridor is not a wall splitting east from west but an axis joining both sides. Five east–west seaming passages cross the axis, all with accessible continuity [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-X1] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]; north–south travel runs on the calibration greenway as the slow-traffic trunk, with low-speed shuttles connecting rail stations to the three calibration grounds [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-TRUNK]. Municipal and new infrastructure follow "institution first, hardware second": power and data networks support temporary power isolation for reversible pilots [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Rail, utility lines and energy-load engineering estimates fall outside this conceptual proposal and remain "for professional deepening".

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space and Urban Character

The central green corridor is a continuous park running the full length of the site — the trunk of the Y and the carrier of the BM milestones: 64 conceptual milestone markers at sleeper rhythm, recording "what was calibrated here, by whom, and with what result" [metric:milestone_marker_count]. The public-space system has three levels: BM nodes (origin plaza, Zhongzhiyuan calibration court, Dazhongsi launch court plus two milestone plazas) [metric:benchmark_node_count], the central public band, and neighbourhood squares [data:geometry/public_space.geojson]. The character language borrows Jing-Zhang engineering aesthetics — track, switch, sleeper, milestone, bench mark — not glass curtain walls and neon: the design goal is replaceable technology and public space that can be maintained over the long term [depth:blue_green_public_space] [metric:landmark_count].

### Public-Space Component Kit

![Public-space component kit](assets/figures/public-space-kit.en.png)

BM0 public space is not a fixed place designed once and for all, but a modular component system that is composable, replaceable and scalable. Six modules correspond to the different scales of the Y's three ends and two wings; each module has a defined size range, applicable scene and combination logic [depth:public_space_component_kit] [metric:public_space_module_count]:

| Community Plaza | 15–30 m side | West living-band blocks | Embedded; number expandable |

The component-kit design principle: the design goal is replaceable technology and public space that can be maintained over the long term. Replacing any single module must not shake the overall framework of the Y structure and the calibration protocol.

### Phasing and conversion path

- **Phase 1 · Set datum** (Origin community): institution first, landmark second. P1/P2 lead, placing the institutional outcome "standards made public" ahead of any hardware [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-1].
- **Phase 2 · Diverge & trial** (Zhongzhiyuan + axis): P3–P6 turn the validation grounds and the green corridor into reversible pilots, trial and repair as you go.
- **Phase 3 · Maintain & launch** (Dazhongsi + whole line): P7–P9 carry premieres, soft landing and long-term operation [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-3].

The conversion path is a closed loop: visitor → join the open problem call → enter residency/evaluation → pass calibration → premiere → become part of the maintenance log → attract the next visitor [depth:phasing_implementation] [depth:renewal_project_list]. Every pilot facility has explicit acceptance and deactivation thresholds; there is no "enter-only, never-exit" commitment.
